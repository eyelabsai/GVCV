The VAULT Method for ICL Sizing

An AI, best-fit-per-eye approach to EVO ICL sizing · by Gurpal Virdi, MD

The VAULT Method is an AI approach to sizing the EVO ICL that predicts the best-fitting lens for an individual eye from anterior-segment imaging, rather than choosing a size from white-to-white diameter and a single formula. It is built on the peer-reviewed VAULT and VAULT-OCT research and a dataset of 756 real ICL surgical cases, and implemented in ICL Fit.

What is ICL vault, and why does sizing matter?

Vault is the gap between the ICL and your natural lens. Too high can crowd the eye's drainage angle and raise pressure; too low can sit close to the natural lens. Because the same eye can vault very differently across the four available lens lengths (12.1, 12.6, 13.2, 13.7 mm), choosing the size is the single most consequential decision in ICL surgery.

Why not size an ICL to a target vault number?

Sizing to hit one universal vault number treats every eye as average. The VAULT Method instead predicts the size most likely to sit well for that specific eye's anatomy — best fit, not a target number. Vault is a readout of fit, not the goal itself.

How is the VAULT Method different from traditional sizing formulas?

Traditional sizing relies on white-to-white corneal diameter and a single formula or nomogram — the OCOS (STAAR) nomogram, the Reinstein formula, the KS formula (Kamiya–Shoji), the NK formula (Nakamura), and the Parkhurst nomogram. Each uses an external proxy for the internal space where the lens actually rests. The VAULT Method predicts fit directly from anterior-segment imaging features — such as angle-to-angle distance, anterior chamber depth, and lens rise — using machine learning trained on real surgical outcomes, rather than one fixed regression on corneal diameter.

What is the VAULT Method built on?

It is grounded in two peer-reviewed studies in the Journal of Cataract & Refractive Surgery and a real-world dataset of 756 EVO ICL surgical cases:

  • VAULT-OCT: Predicting ICL Postoperative Vault from AS-OCT Using Deep Learning
    Journal of Cataract & Refractive Surgery (JCRS), 2025
    DOIPubMed

Who developed the VAULT Method?

It was developed by Gurpal Virdi, MD and Matt Hirabayashi, MD — ophthalmologists and ICL-sizing researchers, and co-founders of ICL Fit, an AI ICL-sizing agent. Dr. Virdi trained at Parkhurst NuVision under Dr. Greg Parkhurst, of the Parkhurst nomogram, one of the most-published EVO ICL teams in the U.S.
